I found Randy Pausch's story to be extremely inspiring. It goes to show that no matter what the world throws at you, life is what you make of it.
I've often wondered what I'd do if I found out I don't have long to live, in my head that's often looked like going into massive credit card debt,
just living a life of excess until I pass. So I found it very cool how rather than panicing Randy instead chose to spend his remaining time puting good into the world.

I really identified with Randy's brick wall theory, that the brick walls in life aren't there to keep us out but rather to let us prove how badly we want something.
I related to this because in my personal life I've kind of hit a brick wall that's shown me I maybe don't want something as much as I thought I did. So in my opinion brick walls can not only push us
to improve ourselves to achieve the things we really want, but act as a guiding force to stop us from overly commiting to things that deep down we don't.

Overall I truly enjoyed learning about Randy's views on life and his own path, I can only imagine how fun of a Professor he was.
