I believe learning styles absolutely impact how we learn. Personally I have always struggled with lecture style learning. 
For whatever reason my brain zones out whenever someone is trying to teach through speach, meanwhile if I just watch someone do something I can learn it very quickly. 
The hard part of this for educators is that while they lose students like me while lecturing, it works for others. Meanwhile those same people may be lost while demonstrating how to do something without enough verbal instruction.
Everyone is different, there is no universal strategy to get every student to grasp what you're teaching. Personally I don't think there's a solution, everyone just has to do their best to try and make it work.
